How to play

KOTOR 1 and KOTOR 2,
with Jedi Academy combat.

kjk is a free, open-source engine that plays both Knights of the Old Republic games with real-time movement, lightsabers and Force powers. Up to three friends can play the story together.

Download the launcher

For Windows, macOS and Linux. You need your own copies of the games.

How to play

  1. Install the games.You need KOTOR, KOTOR II and Jedi Academy. Copies from Steam or GOG are found automatically. kjk reads the games from these installs, and no game data is included in any of its downloads.
  2. Download the kjk launcher.Pick the file for your computer below and unpack it.
  3. Open the launcher and press Play.The launcher finds your games, downloads the newest version of kjk and keeps it up to date.
Windows64-bit, .zip macOSApple silicon, .tar.gz Linux64-bit, .tar.gz

The downloads are not signed, so your computer warns you the first time you open the launcher. On Windows, click "More info", then "Run anyway". On macOS, open System Settings, then Privacy & Security, and click "Open Anyway".

Movies need ffmpeg. kjk runs without it, but skips the movies.

To count your play time in Steam, open the launcher's Settings page and copy the line under "Steam play time" into the game's launch options in Steam.

The launcher is optional. Every version of kjk can also be downloaded on its own from the kjk releases page.
