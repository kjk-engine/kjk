# Enhanced Lightmaps: decisions and experiments

This is the record of the decisions and experiments behind Enhanced Lightmaps. The work is pull request 576, on the branches `feat/fitted-lights` and `feat/placeable-light`. Each entry says what was tried or decided, what came of it with its numbers, why, and whether it is kept, dropped or open. Commit hashes are given where they are known. The owner's words are quoted with their dates.

The plan behind this work is `docs/LIGHTING-PLAN.md`, steps 7 to 10b. This log does not repeat the plan. It holds the results and the reasons the plan leaves out.

The review tools are not on this branch. These are the picture harness, its shots, the review page and the round scripts. The old hand-over notes are not on this branch either. All of them are kept on the branch `chore/light-review-harness`.

## Words used here

- A fitted light is a light the game puts at a lamp the level art shows, such as a glowing tube, panel, screen or sign.
- A kind is one glowing texture and the rule for its light.
- The stored lightmaps are BioWare's lightmaps in the player's game files.
- A bake writes two sets of lightmaps. Set `a` mixes the stored lightmaps with the lamps. Set `b` holds the lamps, the sun, the sky and their bounced light alone. Only set `b` is shipped.
- A levels file gives each room the level its baked lamps are scaled to.
- The fill is light that a room takes from its own stored lightmap when its lamps cannot reach its level.
- The even light is a flat light that a bake gave every texel of such a room, before the fill existed.
- A round is one labelled run of bakes, such as `g6`, `b1`, `r1` or `r2`.
- "Today" is the game with the stored lightmaps and no fitted lights.

## Fitting lights

- A survey verb, `kjk room-lights`, read every lamp and glowing surface of both games. No kind of lamp sits clearly in a bright patch of the lightmap. The shadow-flagged room lamps come nearest, with a median ratio of 1.22 in KOTOR 1 and 1.39 in KOTOR 2. The survey is finished and kept on `feat/room-lights`, in `room_lights.rs`.
- The survey asked whether glowing surfaces can stand in for missing lamps. Panels that look like lights exist in 40 KOTOR 1 areas and 24 KOTOR 2 areas where a lightmapped room has no lamp. They cover 19% and 4% of the lightmapped rooms. The rule for "looks like a light" is that a tenth of the texels are brighter than 0.75. That rule is a guess.
- Self-illumination was found to be mostly not a light. Doors alone have 5,544 glowing meshes in KOTOR 1 and 3,463 in KOTOR 2. Leaves, cliffs and floors glow too. This is why a glowing floor, wall or trim is no lamp in the later rules.
- The original game was run with the K1R mod's Glow Rod to learn what a type 2 light does. It lights characters and placeables and leaves walls and floors dark. kjk does the same under Classic. Under Enhanced a carried light lights the rooms as a fight's light does. This is kept (#457).
- The strengths of the files' lights were fitted to BioWare's lightmaps by least squares. The fit scored R² 0.41 in the cantina, 0.32 in the Ebon Hawk and 0.09 in the Leviathan hall. Random controls scored 0.14, 0.15 and 0.07. Half of the light or more stayed in a flat term. So a bake from the files' lights would look unlike BioWare's. The trial ended on `feat/room-lights`, in `room_light_fit.rs`.
- In that fit the shadow-flagged lamps carry almost none of the bake. Alone they score 0.01, 0.01 and 0.00. One bounce added 0.01 to 0.02 to every score, and the controls gained as much. The best falloff was 1/d ending at the radius in two areas.
- The owner read steps 7a, 7c and 7d on 14 November 2026. He decided that the stored lightmaps stay as the base. Ambient occlusion came next as step 8. This held until the fitted-lights bake of step 10b.
- A search for lights the files do not list was tried in the cantina with `room-light-fit --free`. With 160 free lights R² rose from 0.41 to 0.61. The flat term's share fell from 50% to 12%. The first bake was too light in the dark, off by 28.7 of 255. Weighing dark texels (`--free-dark 1`) brought that to 23.0. This experiment is kept on `feat/detail-bake`, draft pull request #499, and is not to be merged.
- Lamps painted into the lightmaps as bright texels were added to the search as panels. The cantina has 579 of them. R² rose to 0.55, and the mean difference fell to 22.1 of 255. The owner had seen that the strips at the foot of the bar were wrong. This is part of the same experiment on `feat/detail-bake`.
- The found lights were tried as the area's lamps for shadows (`KJK_FOUND_LIGHTS`). With all 96 lights the shadows were as faint as on main. Merged into 33 lights by 5 m cell, the shadows showed. Later the files' lamps and the sun stayed, and the found lights only added. The owner judged stored, found, and found with Ultra very similar. So the found lights count as groundwork only.
- The search for found lights was ruled out outdoors. In the Dantooine courtyard the lamps explained the lightmaps no better than the control, so the sun always stays. The search took 40 minutes on Ahto City. The reason was not found.
- The owner then asked for lights at the lamps a player can see in the level art. This was the start of the fitted lights on `feat/fitted-lights`, pull request 576.
- `data/lights/k1.toml` and `data/lights/tsl.toml` hold one generated entry for each kind. `tools/light-kinds/kinds.py` writes them. `data/lights/fittings.toml` holds the rules' numbers, 43 exceptions with reasons and 24 hand-placed lights. This is kept.
- The owner reviewed the kinds from sheets of before and after pictures. He said "i like all the lights pretty much now" of KOTOR 1 on 16 November 2026. He said "ok looks good" of KOTOR 2 on 17 November 2026. He asks for ready proposals with one "wrong" button and will not fill in forms.
- The owner gave rules for every kind on 16 November 2026. Every glowing fitting gives at least a little light. The room keeps about its level. A light shines out of its fitting without washing it out. Reach stays narrow. Screens, signs, crystals, fires and kolto tanks give a faint light. A mesh of several lamps gets one light on each. A lamp's light should read as a pool near the lamp. These rules are kept.
- A survey field, `bright_mates`, finds lamps from bright texels. Applied to every kind it changed about 30 approved KOTOR 1 kinds. So it stays opt-in with `"lamps": "bright"`. The owner left the choice to the agent.
- The survey found 57 more kinds by colour (`by_colour`). They are proposals in the last block of `fittings.toml`. The owner has seen none of them, so they are open.
- `crates/game/src/fitted_lights.rs` finds the lamps of each glowing mesh when an area loads and puts a light on each. `KJK_FITTED_LIGHTS=on` turns the live lights on under Enhanced. Without the switch the game looks as before. This is kept (commit ff16e127).
- The fitted lights first switched on by camera distance. The owner said "all lamps are not activated when i look down this hall", and his graphics card's fans ran loud. The lights now sit in a fixed grid over each area's floor plan (`fitted_lights::grid`). No light switches with the camera. This is kept.

The constants are `LEVEL_OVER` 1.1 and `LEVEL_LIFT` 0.56. `LEVEL_HOT` falls from 1.1 to 0.7 in rooms that are bright today. The owner walked the Endar Spire with it and said "works! and endar spire looks SO good". This is kept.

The visible fittings carry the room's light, and the hidden lamps' fill stays low beside them. The owner said "i expect them to clearly be the illuminaryies". This is kept.

The owner had said a terminal "is not emitting any light" and that the halls and doors were too bright. He approved these in his next walk.

## The baker

- A detail bake was tried that keeps BioWare's lightmaps and adds detail at four times the size with no lights. It changed the picture by about 1 of 255, which cannot be seen. A plain occlusion version was visible but darkened the lamp recesses, which is not BioWare's light. Both were dropped. They are on `feat/detail-bake`, draft pull request #499.
- A bake from the step 7c fitted lights was tried in the cantina (`room-light-fit --bake`). The lights alone made the room an even grey-blue with no shadows, and the owner said they "look blocky". BioWare's lightmaps times their shadow edges looked the same as stored. It was dropped.
- The games' lightmap layouts were measured. Every face is its own island. 82% of the islands are one texel across. A texel covers 50 to 70 cm. A lightmap four times larger keeps its coordinates and leaves about 2 spare texels between islands. So the bake writes four texels to each stored texel's side (`SCALE`).
- The first bake from the fitted lights ran on the CPU in 23 minutes for the Endar Spire. Set A was the stored bake halved plus the lights. Set B was the lamps alone. A came out about 13% darker than live, and B looked nearly the same as A.
- The owner decided that the shipped bake is set B, made from the lamps alone. He said "we have to do B so we can actually ship it". No pixel of BioWare's lightmaps goes into it. This is kept.
- The bake moved to the graphics card with wgpu ray queries (`detail_bake/gpu.rs`, `gpu.wgsl`). The first GPU bake of the Endar Spire took 10.8 s against 23 minutes on the CPU, on 18 November 2026. Commit ff16e127 gives about 12 s, and later runs took about 12 to 13 s. The CPU bake's blocky shadow on the bridge's slanted panels was gone. This is kept (commit ff16e127).
- The bake on the CPU was removed on 24 November 2026, at the owner's request. Every shipped set was made on the graphics card, by `tools/light-kinds/canon/canon.sh` with `--gpu`. The graphics card's bake writes every file of a set: the PNGs of `a` and `b`, `fill.txt`, `floor.txt`, `probes.txt`, the probes' sun file, `placeables.txt` and `fingerprint.txt`. The CPU bake wrote only the PNGs of `a` and `b`, with one bounce for `a` and two for `b`. The detail bake without lights and `room-light-fit --bake` went with it, since both were dropped above. Removed with them: `--old`, which made the graphics card copy the CPU bake ray for ray, and the flags of the detail bake (`--rays`, `--strength`, `--width`, `--plain`, `--near`). `--gpu` is still taken, so older command lines keep working, and `--lights` is now needed for a bake. The graphics card's bake still finds the texels and reads the lights on the CPU, with the CPU's ray grid of the rooms (`light_probes::Level`). A bake of `tar_m02af` after the removal matched the shipped set file for file.
- The bake's light sets its own rules. Each light casts 64 soft shadow rays. The lamp's glass casts no shadow. A light reaches everything within 0.5 m. Set B has three bounces. This is kept.
- The bake was measured against the live lights in the Endar Spire's hall and bridge. The opaque pass took 0.76 and 0.97 ms with no fitted lights. It took 1.05 and 1.24 ms with the live lights and 0.79 and 1.00 ms with the bake. So the live lights cost about 0.27 ms and the bake about 0.03 ms. This makes the bake the path for weaker graphics cards.
- Set `a` mixes in the stored lightmaps and must never be given to players. Only set `b` matters.
- Set `b` is scaled room by room to a levels file. At first a room that would need more than `B_MOST` 6 times its light took an even light at `B_FILL_SHARE` 0.4 of its level. Since 18 November 2026 the even light gives all that the lamps cannot. This fixed black floors in Peragus (commit d3f849c8).
- The level a room aims at is `lit`. It is the room's stored mean with each texel weighed by its own level. A bake at the plain mean spread light into black corners and looked darker. In the enclave the stored lightmaps shown through the baked path read 41 against 42 today. The plain mean bake read 31, and the `lit` bake 43. This is kept, with `--weigh`.
- Each module has its own level in `data/lights/bake.toml`, and `tools/light-kinds/bake.sh` reads it. The owner asked "we should be able to have different levels of light/darkness in different modules". The game does not read the file. This is kept (commit f5cb1d01).
- The approved areas are held to levels files the owner approved, in `data/lights/levels/`. These are the Harbinger, the Endar Spire and the enclave. He said "Looks good" of the Harbinger's bake and "looks good!" of the Endar Spire's. Their files must not be made again from the live lights. This is kept (commits f5cb1d01, 8c50de1d).
- After every change the approved areas were baked again and compared to the last pixel. Rounds `g6` to `g15`, `h2` to `h8` and `a10` to `a12` matched. The one exception was Peragus in `g6`. There two small rooms on the even light, `103pero` and `101peras`, changed by up to 8 of 255, and their levels rose from 0.040 to 0.099 and from 0.086 to 0.110. Nobody has looked at those two rooms in the game. This check is the working method.
- A bake may have a sun and a sky, set in `bake.toml`, since the games' files hold neither. `--sun-fit` prints the ways to the sun that fit the stored lightmaps best. On the Mac it took 73 s for unk_m44ac and read 1,061,437 texels under the open sky. The sun and the sky are not scaled to a room's level. Only the lamps are. This is kept (commit f5cb1d01).
- The sun's colours come from a recipe. The stored levels are taken to the power 2.2. Each sun's colour is half the difference between the sunlit and shaded ground, over the sun's height. The sky is 1.6 times the shaded ground. A factor of 1.3 applies on Tatooine and 1.0 elsewhere. The recipe gives Anchorhead's own numbers back from Anchorhead's stored levels.
- Skylights were tried in the enclave (`bake/s5`) and taken out. The owner wanted "the skylight come down in the middle and scatter" and less artificial light. The `skylights` key still works, and no module uses it.
- An opening in a roof shines down. A room is under a roof when less than `ROOFED_FROM` 0.1 of its upward texels see more than `OPENING_TO` 0.2 of the sky. Such a room gets `ROOF_GAIN` 8 times the light that falls on the opening. In a courtyard a shaded texel takes `OPENING_GAIN` 4 times. The owner asked to "shine down even more light here from the skylight". The enclave was approved at `bake/s9` with "approved!" on 19 November 2026. This is kept (commit f5cb1d01).
- The enclave's 14 views have a mean brightness of 42 today, 33 in `bake/s8` and 36 in `bake/s9`. The training room is still under today's level, at 47 against 69. The second courtyard is brighter than today, at 52 against 34. The light from the openings is yellow-green beside today's grey, because it has the sun's colour. The owner has not spoken about these.
- `even = false` takes the even light away from a module (`--no-even`). The enclave uses it, because the owner wanted rooms "naturally dark in the sides". The halls without an opening stayed about half as bright as today, with every lamp at its most scale of 6. The owner has not said whether that is wanted.
- Grass takes its light from the walkmesh's lightmap texels, which no drawn mesh covers. The bake left them black, and the owner said "grass is black here". The bake now fills them. The enclave has 26,278 such texels (`grass_texels`). This is kept (commit f5cb1d01).
- With a sun, every room with a lightmapped mesh is baked (`every_room`), not only rooms with a walkable face. Anchorhead had 140 lightmaps in such rooms. They showed at twice their stored light beside the baked ground. The owner had seen "large squares of ground in different light/colors". A room with no walkable face also holds the far land, with triangles kilometres long, so only its lightmapped meshes are read. This is kept (commit 8c50de1d).
- A sun entry may name a second sun. Anchorhead's two suns stand where its two 18,000 m hidden lights point. With one sun the owner said the shadows were not "aligned with the actual suns". This is kept (commit 8c50de1d).
- Anchorhead was shown at two strengths. `bake/a5` had the suns at the stored level, with a mean of 90 over 18 views against 88 today. `bake/a6` had the colours at 1.3 times that, with a mean of 104. The walked `bake/a4` had been at 138. The owner said of the brighter one "overall brightness is good now!", so the factor 1.3 stays.
- A sun may have a `width`. Each texel then casts 64 rays from a grid over its square (`SHARP_SUN_RAYS`). The old sun was 0.04 radians wide, about ten times the real sun, and its shadows were blurred or stepped. Anchorhead has 0.01. The owner chose `bake/a8` on 19 November 2026 with "i pref a8. its decent although some weird shadows remain" (commit 8c50de1d).
- A trial with half the sky's light and stronger suns (`bake/a9`) was made for more directional light. The mean of 26 views went from 102 to 99. The owner chose `a8` over it.
- The Dune Sea (`tat_m18aa`) has no lightmaps, so there is nothing to bake. All 516 meshes of its dump have no lightmap. Its 33 fitted lights are all path markers. `tat_m18ab` has no lightmaps either. A bake there would need new lightmap coordinates or live sun shadows. This is open.
- The Sand People Enclave (`tat_m20aa`) showed that one sun setting does not serve all of Tatooine. Anchorhead's setting made it 80 against 47 today, with shadows falling the wrong way. Its two suns were measured from pictures of its sky, about 53 degrees to the right of Anchorhead's. `bake/e5` reached 58. Its middle room under the tents is at 0.29 against a target of 0.42. Giving its suns a `width` of 0.025 later changed nothing in the pictures.
- A first batch baked every remaining module with settings by rule on 19 and 20 November 2026. It tried the 167 modules that have lightmaps and had no bake. 164 baked, and 3 did not. A module took a sun with at least 20,000 open texels, a fit of 0.4 and a sunlit level 0.08 over the shaded one. 63 modules took one. Of the 164, 129 came out within 0.7 to 1.4 times today's brightness, 27 brighter and 8 darker. The numbers are of four views at the entry point and are a rough guide.
- The first batch's outliers showed two faults of the rule. About 11 indoor modules with windows to space passed the sun rule and were washed grey-white or went dark. About 19 dark modules were filled flat and grey by the even light, and the red rooms of `853nih`, `904mal` and `001ebo` lost their red. `tar_m08aa` was washed white, at 101 against 59.
- A second round dropped the even light and needed 100,000 open texels for a sun. Five modules were marked indoors by hand, because no number tells a dusk area from a cave. 42 modules took a sun. 113 modules were in range, 12 brighter and 38 darker. Modules with few lamps, such as Malachor and the Korriban tombs, went to a fifth to a half of today. The owner had chosen "No even light".
- A third round added `--fill-stored`, `--most` and `--least`. A room its lamps cannot light takes its own stored light for the rest, so it keeps its colour. Many dark rooms had been held at the least scale of 0.5 and could not go darker, which is why `--least` exists. The owner said "Yes, write them" on 20 November 2026. The settings are in `bake.toml` (commits 339cff14, 38bd5f23).
- The Ebon Hawk was bright because its hold's level counts a few bright texels that no lamp reaches. Its lamps went to six times their strength. It now bakes at share 0.3 with `--most 0.4` and `--least 0.1`, at 32 against 19 today (commit 38bd5f23).
- The fill of a lampless room comes from the player's own files. The bake writes the room's lamps alone and a `fill.txt` with one number for each lightmap. The game adds the stored lightmap times that number when it loads (`add_stored_fill`). The owner asked on 21 November 2026 "do we need to hold bioware lightmap pixels?" and said "yes" to building it (commit 951fab66).
- The fill from the player's files was checked against the older mixed bake in `902mal` and `107per`. The pictures differed by 0.6 to 1.9 of 255, and only at the seams between a lightmap's patches. The grid lines that the owner saw in `604dan`, `702kor` and `710kor` came from the older mixed fill, and a fresh bake has none.

The key was applied to every module at first. Round r1 showed that it darkened walked and liked modules, such as Davik's estate at 54 against 107. So it is opt-in. Any general change must be checked against every walked module's brightness.
- Round r1 baked all 171 walked modules again after the KOTOR 1 and KOTOR 2 notes. None failed, and it took about six hours. The rooms with no walkable face were moved out of 33 modules' sets and shot again.
- Round r2 baked only the 44 modules the owner commented on, at his request. It is commit 514100cc.
- A blended room surface with a lightmap now shows its bake at full light (`ROOM_LIGHTMAP`). Before, only an opaque room surface took the bake's gain, so a blended one showed a quarter of its light. Dxun's boulders (`401dxn`) and the enclave's shrubs (`605dan`) had been black. This is round r2 (commit 514100cc).
- A texel counts as inside a wall only if the backs it meets are within `WALL_NEAR` 1 m. Rays from the sand of `tat_m20aa` met the tents' cloth overhead, and whole triangles of sand were left out. This is round r2 (commit 514100cc).

## Distribution and patches

- How the lightmaps ship was undecided until 23 November 2026. One earlier reading was that the game would bake on the player's machine. The decision is a download of ready bakes, made by the developers.
- A bake's probe and placeable files hold no light from the games' lightmaps. The baker writes a word such as `@0:244581` that names the texel the fill came from. The game reads that texel of the player's own lightmap (`stored_fill.rs`). In `411dxn` the chests hold 0.0007 to 0.0015 in the file against 0.19 to 0.24 before, and the game draws them as before. This lets a bake be given out as a download (commit 6f6f1b28).
- The bake's format is 2. The game takes nothing from a `placeables.txt` or probe file without a `format` line, and every older set must be baked again. In `411dxn` the new `placeables.txt` grew from 356 KB to 456 KB and `probes.txt` from 1.91 MB to 2.04 MB. This is kept (commit 6f6f1b28).
- Each bake carries a fingerprint of the files it was made from (`bake_fingerprint.rs`). It has a digest of the rooms, a digest of the placeables it lights and a format number. TSLRCM changes the placeables of about 11 areas and the rooms of 2. The K1 Community Patch changes the rooms of 18 areas and the placeables of about 37 modules. - When the rooms or the format differ, the game draws the area with its own lightmaps and takes nothing from the set. ## Cost under Classic and Enhanced

- The fitted lights and the trials cost GPU time under Classic and plain Enhanced, although none of their code ran there. Under Classic the Harbinger's medbay took 1.80 ms of GPU time against 1.41 ms on the merge base. The crowd brawl's particles took 0.71 ms against 0.14 ms. Leaving out only the calls to `fitted_lights`, or only the trials' code, each brought back about half. Leaving out both brought back all of it.
- The shaders now hold that code only in a run that may show it (`FITTED_LIGHTS`, `fitted_lights::in_shaders`). That is a run with Enhanced Lightmaps chosen and installed, `KJK_FITTED_LIGHTS` set to anything but `off`, `KJK_SHADOW_DEBUG=7`, `KJK_FOUND_LIGHTS` or `KJK_MODEL_LIGHT`. Classic then cost what it cost on the merge base, in the medbay, the upper cantina, the crowd brawl and the refugee brawl. The baked look of `305nar` stayed the same, with a mean difference of 0.012 to 0.026 of 255 (commit e5af06ca).
- A light's cone and the sun's share in the room shadows came only from the trials, and they cost plain Enhanced about 0.02 ms in the medbay. They are now part of `FITTED_LIGHTS`. The particles of an emitter are always unlit, so their pipelines leave the trials out under Enhanced Lightmaps too. The crowd brawl's 930 particles then took 0.14 ms there, against 0.75 ms (commit 55c5d0a3).
- A larger light record (`GpuLight::aim`) and the new probe fields cost nothing. The base shader with the branch's buffer layouts ran at the base's speed.
- The refugee brawl seemed to cost about 0.15 to 0.3 ms more than the merge base under Classic and plain Enhanced, with the depth prepass forced the same in both. A search on 24 November 2026 ruled out every difference of the branch it tried: its shader, the light record, the grid binding, the rounded boxes, the light-inside-a-box test, the probes, the store, the scrolling speed and the `ROOM_LIGHTMAP` flag. Draws, shaded pixels and scene facts were the same in both builds.
- The cost was the full NVIDIA shader cache, not the branch. With the cache emptied and both builds loaded once, both finished their pipeline warm-up while loading. The refugee brawl with the full depth prepass then took 4.30 ms of GPU time on the branch against 4.36 ms on the merge base under plain Enhanced (4.20 to 4.34 against 4.26 to 4.41, five alternating runs each). Under Classic it took 3.96 ms against 3.90 ms (3.95 to 3.98 against 3.81 to 3.96). The medbay under plain Enhanced took 1.47 ms against 1.49 ms. The graphics clock was the same in both builds, 2760 MHz while busy in the brawl. The clocks could not be locked, since `nvidia-smi -lgc` needs an administrator.
- Why a build whose shaders cannot enter the cache draws slower is not known. It held even when both builds compiled their shaders fresh. A bisect of the branch's runtime changes is therefore not needed.

## A lost graphics device while pipelines compile

- Some windowed runs end in their first second with "Caught DeviceLost error: Unknown Device is lost", after "TimeSpanScope::end was never called". The backend is Vulkan, on NVIDIA's driver 621.14. Each loss comes with an `nvlddmkm` Event 153 ("Error occurred on GPUID") in the Windows system log at the same second. No Event 4101, a driver reset, was logged.
- It happens only while pipelines compile fresh in the first frames of an area. Then about 100 to 340 pipelines are still compiling while the area is drawn.
- This machine's NVIDIA shader cache is full at 1 GiB. Shaders cached before it filled still load from it. A new shader is compiled again on every run. So the branch's shaders compile fresh each time. The render thread then waits 12.4 s in Bevy's `prepare_view_upscaling_pipelines`, the load takes 13 to 14 s against 5.4 s, and the pipeline pre-warm never finishes before the area is drawn.
- Short loads of the medbay under plain Enhanced, on 24 November 2026, gave these counts. The branch as it is lost the device in 4 of 30 runs. In a second loop it lost it in 3 of 20 runs, 2 of 10 as it is and 1 of 10 with bounds-checked shaders (`KJK_CHECKED_SHADERS=1`). Commit f4dd2a93 lost it in 0 of 30 runs, and in 1 of 30 with ambient occlusion, whose shader variants are not in the cache. The merge base lost it in 0 of 30 runs from the cache. With bounds-checked shaders, which are new and so compile fresh, it lost it in 3 of 10 runs.
- So this branch does not cause the crash. It shows here because the branch's shaders cannot enter the full cache. A player meets the same fresh compile on a first run or after any change of the shaders. The crash also came with bounds-checked shaders, so it is not a read past a list in kjk's own shaders.
- With the cache emptied, the branch loads in 7.4 s and finishes its pipeline warm-up while loading, as the merge base does. A load with the cache emptied before it still compiles every pipeline fresh.
- Loads of the medbay with the cache emptied before each one, on 24 November 2026, gave these counts. With Vulkan and its ray queries the branch lost the device in 3 of 35 runs. With `KJK_RAYS=off` it lost it in 0 of 30 runs. With DX12 (`WGPU_BACKEND=dx12`), which offers no ray queries, it lost it in 0 of 15 runs, and each load took about 90 s. So the loss may come from building the ray-tracing structures while pipelines compile. This is not proven: 0 of 45 against 3 of 35 is a hint, not a measurement.
- Vulkan's validation layer is not installed on this machine, so it was not tried. The machine has three implicit Vulkan layers that load into every Vulkan program: OBS's hook, Steam's overlay and Steam's Fossilize layer.
- The Windows log holds no machine-check or memory error (WHEA events 18, 19 and 47). It holds 499 corrected PCI Express errors (WHEA event 17), all from the graphics card. They come six at a time when the machine starts after an unexpected shutdown (Kernel-Power event 41). Such shutdowns happened on 9, 10, 11, 12, 14, 17 and 18 November 2026. No crash of `rustc` is in the application log.
- The loading screen already waits for the area's pipeline warm-up to finish (`lifecycle_view::PictureWait`, at most 30 s). But the area is drawn behind it in the meantime, and the losses come then. Holding the area back means not drawing the area's cameras, only the warm-up's stand-ins, until the warm-up is quiet. This is open.

## Other

- The HDR frame stays gamma-encoded, decided with the owner on 12 November 2026. Every blend, fog, fade and the HUD keep their look. New light is worked out in linear light inside the surface shader.
- Step 9 made `KJK_LINEAR=temporal` the linear frame, decoded only around the temporal pass. It stays behind its switch, because it costs under 1% of a frame and shows no visible gain yet (#453). The two-texture design must come before any effect drawn as its own pass between the opaque and blended layers.
- Feedback on one area is a rule for every area. The owner wants to tune three or four areas and have the rest done without him. So a fix must be a general rule, not a tweak for one room.
- The working method is to change one rule, rebake the approved areas, shoot the same poses and look at every sheet before showing the owner. A fix for one area often broke another. Peragus's switched lamps washed out a Harbinger shadow.
- Comparison videos for the owner turn slowly, a full turn in 24 s or more. A turn in 12 s was "a bit too fast and intense".
- Every benchmark waits for the owner's go. The approved tests run in the pull request, not locally. Other sessions and the owner's own game share the machine.
- This machine's frame rate is bimodal, about two times apart between runs. Headless GPU times read about three times too high, because the card idles at 60 frames a second. GPU costs are read from windowed runs. GPU savings count even where this machine shows no frame-rate gain.
- A model's light is measured as its mean brightness over the floor just below it. `KJK_MODEL_LIGHT=mask` paints probed models magenta to find their pixels. Two launches of one build differ by about 1 in mean value, so smaller differences are not changes.
