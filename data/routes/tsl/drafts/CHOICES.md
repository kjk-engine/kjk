# TSL chain: the playthrough's choices

The drafts in this folder are routes written from the walkthroughs and the
modules' own data before the chain reaches them. Each one is moved into
`data/routes/tsl/` and run, then fixed, when its milestone comes up. Every
draft follows the choices below, and its header lists any other guess it
makes about the save it starts from.

- **Alignment:** light side throughout. Take the merciful or truthful
  replies; never a lie that wins a dark side point. Persuade only where
  the Exile's ranks allow it (the chain's Exile has no Awareness,
  Computer or Repair ranks to speak of; companions' skills open doors and
  consoles).
- **The Exile:** male, so the Handmaiden joins at Atris's academy (Telos)
  and the Disciple does not.
- **Companions the chain has by the end of Nar Shaddaa:** Atton, Kreia,
  Bao-Dur, T3-M4, the Handmaiden, Mira (Hanharr does not join), G0-T0,
  and Visas Marr (her arrival is flagged; her ambush on the Ebon Hawk may
  still be owed). HK-47 only if his parts were collected; assume he is
  not assembled.
- **Order, DSimpson's:** Peragus, Telos, Dantooine, Nar Shaddaa, Onderon
  and Dxun (Mandalore joins), Korriban, then the finale (Onderon's
  civil war, Dantooine's Enclave, Telos's academy again), the Ravager,
  and Malachor V to the end credits.
- **Story calls on the light side path:**
  - **Onderon:** Queen Talia's side.
  - **Dxun:** Mandalore's tests passed, and Freedon Nadd's tomb resisted.
  - **The finale:** Kreia faced on Malachor and her teaching rejected.
  - **Kreia's influence:** where a conversation weighs it, keep it above
    the line that turns her against the party.
- **Optional content:** only where it costs nothing; note what is skipped.
- **Checkpoints:** `save <name>` at each module boundary or story beat a
  block could be replayed from. Names are `<planet>_<beat>`.
